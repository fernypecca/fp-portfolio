Honest status: Concept exploration. The flow is clickable, not a shipped production tool. This page describes the intended experience.
The problem

One product, three specs, every time.

Merchants need the same product in 1:1, 4:5, 9:16. Plus title, plus description, plus alt text.

Today that means re-briefing the same SKU for each surface. The work repeats. The decisions repeat.

Who it's for

Teams that launch many SKUs.

Marketplace sellers. Small e-commerce teams. Anyone who thinks in channels, not prompts.

The URL is already the source of truth — image, title, category. Start there, finish with a kit.

How it works

Paste once. Use everywhere.

1

Paste a product URL. The page already holds the signals — image, copy, category.

2

Get the kit. Image directions + SEO copy + social assets, bundled together.

3

Ship per channel. 1:1 for social, 4:5 for catalog, 9:16 for stories. No re-brief.

1:1 — Social

Square crop. Alt text and title ready to paste.

4:5 — Catalog

Vertical with space for SEO copy and bullets.

9:16 — Stories

Full vertical. Hook and CTA baked in.

What it demonstrates

Packaging beats generating.

  • URL-to-kit, not prompt-to-image. Starts where the merchant already lives.
  • Channel thinking. Formats reflect distribution specs.
  • One output. Image, SEO, and social together — no re-brief.
