Case study body — two fixes for the same problem

Real Reactor copy, both directions. The problem: on a wide monitor the current pages let the shell stretch to 1440px, but each text-only section caps its reading width way under that — so half the page sits empty. Both variants below fix it, differently.

Variant A — Narrow the whole pageCase-study pages become a focused document: shell caps around 760px regardless of monitor width, so nothing ever floats in empty space. Sections get thin dividers instead. Closest to the quiet, editorial direction you picked for the homepage hero.
